No Personal Data Collected by This Application.
Calculator Popup does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. All data stored by the application remains exclusively on your device and is never sent to our servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how GSCWare ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with your use of Calculator Popup (the "Application"), a floating popup calculator for Android.

By installing or using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please uninstall the Application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The Application does not collect, process, or transmit any of the following types of data:

  • Name, email address, or other contact details
  • Location data (precise or approximate)
  • Camera, microphone, or media access
  • Contacts, calendar, or call logs
  • Biometric data
  • Financial or payment card information
  • Device identifiers (such as IMEI) by our own code

Calculator inputs and results exist solely in device memory during the current session and are never persisted or transmitted.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Application uses Android SharedPreferences to store the following non-personal preferences under the key space calculator_prefs. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted externally by our code.

Key Type Purpose
theme_override Integer Remembers the user's chosen color-scheme mode (system / light / dark).
theme_choice Integer Remembers the selected UI theme style (Default, Minimalist, Material, Flat).
language String Stores the user's preferred display language locale tag.
last_ad_time Long (timestamp) Records the device timestamp of the last interstitial ad display, used purely to enforce a minimum 3-hour interval between ads. No user identity is associated with this value.
launch_count Integer Counts app launches since the last ad was shown, used to decide when to display the next ad. Resets to zero after each ad impression.
is_pro Boolean Caches whether the user has purchased the "Remove Ads" in-app product, so that ad-free status persists across app restarts without querying Google Play Billing on every launch.
debug_override Boolean Developer-only flag for toggling debug mode at runtime. Has no effect in production builds distributed through the Google Play Store.

4. Android Permissions

The Application declares the following permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml. Each permission is required solely for the stated functional purpose and is not used to collect personal data.

Permission Functional Justification
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Draws the floating calculator overlay on top of other applications. This is the core feature of the Application and cannot function without this permission. The user is explicitly prompted to grant it via the Android Settings screen.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps the floating calculator alive while the user is interacting with other apps. Android requires foreground-service permission to run a persistent, visible service.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Required by Android 14+ (API 34) for foreground services that do not fit a predefined category. Declared because the popup-calculator use case is classified as specialUse by the Android platform.
INTERNET Enables the Google AdMob SDK to fetch and display advertisements (banner and interstitial) and enables the Google Play Billing library to verify in-app purchase transactions with Google's servers. The Application itself does not open any other network connections.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Allows the Google AdMob SDK to check network availability before attempting to load ads, preventing unnecessary network errors.
VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback (short vibrations) when the user interacts with the calculator buttons for a more tactile user experience. No data is collected or transmitted.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates the following third-party SDKs. Each third party operates under its own privacy policy, which governs data collected by that SDK independently of our Application.

5.1 Google AdMob

AdMob (provided by Google LLC) is used to display in-app advertisements (banner and interstitial formats) to users who have not purchased the ad-removal option. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance, in accordance with Google's policies.

  • AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use Google services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app product will no longer be served ads, and the AdMob SDK will not be invoked for ad loading.

5.2 Google Play Billing

The Google Play Billing library facilitates the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation of whether the purchase was successfully completed; we do not receive or store any payment card details or billing address information.

  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

5.3 Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC) is used to automatically detect and report application crashes and errors. Crashlytics may collect device state information (device model, OS version, stack traces) at the moment of a crash to help us improve stability. No personally identifiable information is intentionally included in crash reports.

  • Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Third-party data: Data collected by Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Crashlytics is subject to the respective data-retention policies of Google LLC. You may review and manage this data through your Google account settings or by contacting Google directly.

Our servers: We do not operate any backend server that retains user data. There are no accounts, registrations, or user profiles associated with this Application.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information processed by the Application:

  • Local preferences are stored using the Android SharedPreferences API with MODE_PRIVATE, making them accessible only to this Application on a non-rooted device.
  • The Application does not transmit any locally stored data over the network.
  • The Application uses HTTPS for all network communication facilitated by integrated SDKs (AdMob, Google Play Billing, Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Crash report data (via Firebase Crashlytics) is transmitted over encrypted channels to Google's infrastructure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data processed by third-party SDKs.
